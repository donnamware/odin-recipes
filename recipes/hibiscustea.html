Hibiscus Tea

photo by congerdesign

Hibiscus tea is a flavorful tea, that warms the senses. This tea gives me resolve, when I am feeling exhausted.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz of water
  • 2 dried hibiscus flowers
  • (optional) 1 green tea bag
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 3 whole cloves
  • honey
  • stir until well mixed

Steps

  1. boil the water
  2. if you're using a tea bag place it in an 8 oz cup
  3. place the two dried hibiscus flowers in the cup
  4. add honey to the cup, to your preferred taste
  5. add the 3 cloves
  6. pour the hot water over the ingredients in the cup
