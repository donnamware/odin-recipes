Mom's Coffee

photo by StockSnap

This recipe is near to my heart. It is exactly how my mom made her morning coffee. I can still smell the exact aroma that filled the kitchen every morning.

My Mom has been resting in heaven for over 13 years, but the memories of her cooking and coffee will be with me forever!

Ingredients

  • 8 oz of water
  • instant coffee
  • Coffee Mate Original cream (powdered)
  • granulated sugar

Steps

  1. boil water
  2. put two scoops of instant coffee, using a small regular kitchen spoon, into your cup
  3. put two scoops of Coffee Mate Original cream, using the same kitchen spoon, into your cup
  4. put two spoonfuls of granulated sugar, using the same kitchen spoon, into your cup
  5. pour the boiled water over the ingredients
  6. stir until well mixed
