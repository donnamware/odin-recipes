Peanut Butter and Banana Sandwich

no attribution noted

Ingredients

  • 2 thick bread slices
  • Jiffy peanut butter
  • one banana

Steps

  1. spread a generous amount of peanut butter on one side of each slice of bread
  2. peal the banana and cut it in half
  3. Place each half of the banana on top of one peanut butter sided bread slice
  4. put the other slice of bread, peanut butter side down, on top of the bananas
